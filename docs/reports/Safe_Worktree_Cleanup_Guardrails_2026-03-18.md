## Purpose

It is not permission to clean the whole repository.

## Cleanup Rules

1. Do not modify or remove any tracked file.
2. Do not touch product/code/doc feature lanes outside the explicit allowlist below.
3. Do not touch `storage/`, `snapshots/`, `docs/archive/`, `research_dna/`, or any feature surface that may hold valuable local outputs.
4. Prefer local ignore for recurring generated paths.
5. Only remove paths that are:
   - untracked
   - clearly regenerated by tests/tooling
   - low-value temporary artifacts
6. Re-run `git status` after cleanup and stop there. Do not widen scope in the same pass.
